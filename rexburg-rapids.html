Rexburg Rapids Rebrand

Brand Identity & Environmental Graphics

Summer Splash Park Rejuvenation · 2026

The Challenge

Rexburg Rapids had all the essentials of a community waterpark, but the existing brand felt dated and disconnected from the high-energy, sun-soaked atmosphere of the facility.

The Solution

A vibrant visual overhaul centering around motion, splash curves, and high-contrast primary tones across digital touchpoints, facility signage, guest merchandise, and seasonal collateral.
