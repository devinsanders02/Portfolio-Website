01 · Hierarchy · KDS-9

The title is read first: big and red. Then “dual system pistol cleaning kit”, then the product, with tags underneath. Body copy is the thinnest thing on the image, so it’s read last.

Hover to inspect
02 · Specs at a glance

I wanted to pull out important specs that shoppers needed to know and position it in a way that is easy to glance at and read, and making it fast. Fewer words means more of a chance they keep scrolling.

Hover to inspect
03 · Lifestyle

A lifestyle photo with dark callout boxes. It breaks up a run of product-on-white images so the set doesn’t feel the same all the way through.
