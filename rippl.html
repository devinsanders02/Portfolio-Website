RIPPL Branded Hoodie

Pattern System, Apparel Mockups & Brand Merchandise · 2026

The Concept

A custom merchandise concept designed for RIPPL Marketing. Taking the dynamic chevron angles of the RIPPL logo mark, the project creates an all-over tonal pattern that elevates standard company swag into premium streetwear.

The Detail

Engineered with high-contrast electric blue accents, custom hood lining stripes, subtle watermarked geometric overlays (#F9F9F9), and interior screenprinted neck tags.
