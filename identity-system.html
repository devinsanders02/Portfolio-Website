A personal identity for a designer, built around one idea: the work is never finished.

The wordmark

The wordmark ends in an ellipsis, a “to be continued”. It is a mark for someone who is always working on what comes next.

The stationery takes it into print: Marathon Red, black and white, a strict grid and a monospace for the small details.

The full suite

01 · Business cards

A red textured front, and a clean monospace back on the grid.

02 · Envelope

Built around opening it. The red inner flap hides the line “plenty more in the works…”.

03 · Invoice & letterhead

The everyday paperwork on the same grid, so even an invoice looks like the brand.

Standards

Three colours, nothing else

  • Marathon Red#E63946 · PMS 1795 C
  • Black#000000 · Process black
  • White#FFFFFF · Bright uncoated
