Translating Retro Tech into Modular Surface Design

Analog Reboot

12-Surface Pattern System & Apparel Identity · 2026

The Collection

12 Patterns · One Cohesive System

Analog Reboot explores rhythm, retro electronics iconography, and bold neon color blocking through 12 modular surface patterns designed for apparel and lifestyle merchandise.

The Solution

Each repeating graphic derives from vintage tech—cassette ribbons, circuit traces, signal waves, and floppy disks—unified by a calibrated six-color palette and structured typography.
