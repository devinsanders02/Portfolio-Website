Twelve surface patterns sampled from old electronics, made into a small apparel and accessories line.

Where it comes from

Cassette tape, circuit boards and CRT scan lines, turned into repeats that can run across a jacket, a hat or a pair of socks.

Each pattern has its own code, so the set works like a collection.
