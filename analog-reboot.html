Translating Retro Tech into Modular Surface Design

Analog Reboot

12-Surface Pattern System & Apparel Identity · 2026

The Collection

12 Patterns · One Cohesive System

Analog Reboot explores rhythm, retro electronics iconography, and bold color blocking through 12 modular surface patterns designed for apparel, accessories, and print merchandise.

The Foundation

Each repeating graphic derives from vintage tech—cassette ribbons, circuit traces, CRT signal sweeps, and floppy disks—unified by a calibrated six-color palette and high-impact Barlow typography.
