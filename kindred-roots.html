Designing Editorial Space for Human Connection

Kindred Roots Magazine

Editorial Architecture, Typography & Publication Layout · 2026

Editorial Statement // Human Friction

"A study on physical print as an intentional counterweight to frictionless screen consumption."

Kindred Roots explores community, belonging, and the spaces between people in an increasingly isolated culture. This project centered on crafting an intentional editorial architecture that balances bold display typography with spacious, quiet reading spreads—establishing clear grid pacing and warm earthy tones so each multi-page narrative develops its own distinct atmosphere.
