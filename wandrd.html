WANDRD makes camera and travel bags. At RIPPL I designed their Amazon Brand Store, their brand story and the infographics that sell the bags.

The problem with Amazon

Amazon’s layout modules are rigid, and they flatten every brand into the same grid. The job was to work inside those limits with lifestyle tiles, bold type and infographics that still feel like WANDRD.

What I did
  1. Designed the Amazon Brand Store: 15 pages, live now
  2. Designed listing infographics, including the live Rogue V2 set
  3. Designed the brand story that runs on their listings
  4. Worked with WANDRD’s creative team through approvals and critiques
