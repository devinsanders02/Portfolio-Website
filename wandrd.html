WANDRD makes travel and camera bags for creatives. At RIPPL I designed their Amazon Brand Store, and the graphics that sell the bags inside it.

The problem with Amazon

Amazon’s layout modules are rigid, and they flatten every brand into the same grid. The job was to work inside those limits with lifestyle tiles, bold type and infographics that still feel like WANDRD.

What I did
  1. Designed the Amazon Brand Store, which is live now
  2. Created the product infographics for the listings
  3. Developed brand storytelling and supporting e-commerce graphics
