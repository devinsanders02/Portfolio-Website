Segmenting Daily Wellness Across Three Generations

OLLY Vitamin Mix-Ins

Structural Packaging, Print Production & Prototyping · 2026

The System

A conceptual packaging line exploring how OLLY's accessible wellness brand could expand into single-serve powder drink mix-ins. The collection is engineered for three distinct demographics—Kids, Young Adults, and Seniors—each brought to life as fully realized 3D physical mockups.

The Execution

While maintaining a consistent box dieline and iconic OLLY brand architecture, each demographic tier employs tailored color palettes, custom top lid patterns, and targeted benefit hierarchy to speak authentically to its audience.
