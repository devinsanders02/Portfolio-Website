Segmenting Daily Wellness Across Three Generations

OLLY Vitamin Mix-Ins

Structural Packaging, Print Production & Prototyping · 2026

The Expansion System

A conceptual packaging line exploring how OLLY's accessible wellness brand could expand into single-serve powder drink mix-ins. The collection is engineered for three distinct demographics—Kids, Young Adults, and Seniors—each brought to life as fully realized 3D physical mockups.

Demographic Segmentation

While maintaining a consistent box dieline and iconic OLLY brand architecture, each demographic tier employs tailored color palettes, custom top lid patterns, and targeted benefit hierarchy to speak authentically to its audience.

Strawberry Kids Mix-In

Tier 01 // Kids Formula

Vibrant magenta and playful fruit character illustration designed to make daily vitamins engaging for children while highlighting key developmental benefits for parents.

Boost Bandit Fruit Punch

Tier 02 // Young Adult & Active

High-voltage orange paired with low-poly facet geometry on the lid, geared toward students and active adults seeking sustained focus, energy, and physical sharpness.

Prime Care Senior Wellness

Tier 03 // Senior Vitality

Calming cerulean blue with organic swirl motifs and enlarged high-contrast typography, engineered for effortless readability and daily joint/vitality support.

Retail Lineup // Generational Family

The Complete Mix-In Lineup

When arranged side-by-side, the collection establishes immediate retail brand recognition through shared dielines and white faceplates, while the lids and flavor ribbons guide shoppers instantly to their demographic formula.
