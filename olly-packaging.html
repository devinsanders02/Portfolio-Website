A concept packaging line: what if OLLY made single-serve drink mix-ins?

01One box

Every box shares the same dieline and the OLLY brand on a white front panel.

02Three audiences

The colour, the lid pattern and which benefits come first change for kids, young adults and seniors.

03Made for real

All three were built as physical mockups, not just flat renders.

01Kids

Strawberry Kids

Bright magenta and a fruit character to make vitamins fun for kids, with the benefits parents look for spelled out on the back.

02Young adults

Boost Bandit Fruit Punch

Loud orange and a faceted lid pattern, for students and active adults who want energy and focus.

03Seniors

Prime Care

Calm blue, swirl patterns and much bigger type, so it is easy to read without glasses.

The lineup

Same box, three audiences

Side by side they read as one family, and the lid colours tell you which one is yours.
