Motion Reel

After Effects · Illustrator · 2026

What’s in it

Brand identity animation, type moving through space, and pacing that follows the beat. Every transition is timed to keep things moving.

How it’s made

After Effects and Illustrator, with a lot of attention on easing curves, kinetic type, logos that morph, and cuts that follow the audio.

Breakdown · the Tron piece

From the animatic

I storyboarded it first, then cut an animatic: a rough pass of still frames to get the timing right before anything moved smoothly. The glow and light effects came last. These four frames are from that animatic stage.
